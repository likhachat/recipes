
# KEKS

## Ingredients

* 3 eggs
* 1 cup sugar
* 1 cup matsoon
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 1 tbsp vinegar
* 61g softened butter
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 2 1/2 cup flour

## Directions

* Mix eggs & sugar in bowl
* Add in matsoon
* Mix baking soda and vinegar on a spoon
* Add to bowl
* Mix 
* Add butter to bowl
* Pour in vanilla
* Mix in flour until consistency of sour cream
* Put into 360F oven for 30-40 min until baked through
